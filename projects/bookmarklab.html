    BookmarkLab

    Organize, deduplicate, and clean your bookmarks with a safe staging and sync model. Privacy-first and 100% local.

    GitHub → Project site →

    The idea

    Bookmark collections grow messy: duplicates, dead links, and tracking junk in every URL. BookmarkLab cleans them up without risk. Every change is staged first, so you preview deletions, moves, renames, and new folders before anything syncs to your real bookmarks.

    What it does

    • Safe stage-and-commit. Review every change before it touches your live bookmarks.
    • Duplicate detection. Normalizes URLs to find duplicates and keeps the oldest or the one you choose.
    • Cleanup tools. Strips tracking parameters, checks link health, and auto-clusters bookmarks by domain or topic.
    • Import and backup. Imports .html and .json bookmark files from any browser and exports a one-click HTML backup.

    Also included

    • Drag-and-drop organization across tree and workbench views
    • Full-text instant search and batch operations
    • Dark and light modes with muted pastel accents
    • Multi-language support and an offline demo preview
